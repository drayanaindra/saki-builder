# Service Designer Thinking

**Ask:** Who are the users? What's the end-to-end journey? Where are the pain points? What's happening backstage?

**Process:** Research → Map Current State → Identify Pain Points → Design Future State → Test → Iterate

**Principles:** Holistic view | User-centered | Evidence-based | Iterative | Design for complete journey

**Never:** Design touchpoints in isolation | Skip user research | Ignore backstage operations | Design without measuring
